The Jozlo guide

Earn screen time with exercise.

A useful first step before the apps you want to open.

An app blocker creates a boundary around distracting apps. Jozlo adds a way to earn access: choose a useful task or exercise, complete it, then take a timed break. You set the goal and reward rather than accepting a fixed exercise-to-minute rate.

Start with one distracting app.

Choose an app you regularly open on impulse. Grant Screen Time permission, create a plan, and select that app. Keep phone calls, maps, work tools, and other essential functions available. Jozlo is a voluntary habit tool and restrictions depend on device permissions and iOS behavior.

Choose a manageable rep goal.

Pick an exercise appropriate for your ability. Start with a small set you can comfortably complete rather than using an ambitious number to make the rule feel strict. Your goal is a routine you can repeat. Movement demonstrations are available before you begin.

Set the minutes you want to earn. Where available, earned-time options let you pair different rep goals with different rewards. The examples shown in the app are choices, not recommended exercise prescriptions.

Let the camera count the set.

Place your iPhone on a stable surface and follow the positioning instructions. Keep the required body parts inside the camera view. Wait for the starting countdown before doing reps. Live counting and spoken feedback help show progress, but counts are estimates and the app does not assess safe form.

Exercise counting defaults to on-device processing. Optional cloud checks work differently and require separate consent and a metered allowance. Read the privacy notice before choosing them.

Use the break you earned.

After verified completion, start the earned break in Jozlo. The plan shows available time and returns to restrictions when the reward ends, subject to iOS callbacks and device settings. Some controls, including additional plans, schedules, and saved earned minutes, require Pro.

Measure your routine honestly.

Look at completed tasks and verified reps alongside available Screen Time reports. A few successful sessions do not prove that total phone usage has fallen. If your usage rises, adjust the reward length or selected apps instead of treating exercise completion as proof of less scrolling.

Add a morning cue.

Jozlo also supports task-based alarms, so a chosen task can become part of your morning routine. Start with a test alarm and keep a backup for time-critical mornings.

Stop exercising if you feel pain or dizziness. Choose a task that fits your ability and surroundings. For setup problems, visit Jozlo support.
