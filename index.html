Before you download

A few useful answers.

What is Jozlo?

Jozlo is an iPhone screen time app blocker that connects access to distracting apps with completing useful tasks or camera-counted exercises. It also includes optional task-based alarms.

How do I earn screen time with exercise?

Create an app plan, choose your exercise goal and reward minutes, then complete your set using live camera verification. The plan unlocks earned access and applies restrictions again after the break, subject to iOS settings and callbacks.

Does Jozlo have an alarm?

Yes. Set an optional alarm and choose the task for your routine. Alarm permission is required, and standard iOS stop controls remain available. See the task alarm guide for setup and limitations.

Does the camera send my exercise video to AI?

On-device exercise counting does not send camera frames to Jozlo's service or OpenAI. Optional cloud/custom checks send sampled images only with separate consent. Optional exercise recording is off by default. The on-device SDK also sends technical diagnostics; see the privacy notice.

Which devices and countries are supported?

The current public release is available on the US App Store for iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Android availability has not been announced. Jozlo is not currently a parent-managed child-control app.

Do I need a subscription?

The current app offers optional monthly and annual Pro subscriptions for additional plans and controls. Check the current feature list, prices, and billing periods in the app and Apple purchase sheet. Optional cloud checks have a separate metered allowance and are not included in Pro.

Why can't I find Jozlo in App Store search?

Use the direct App Store download link. Check that your Apple Account uses the United States storefront, your iPhone meets the iOS requirement, and your Screen Time settings allow the app's current age rating. Search visibility can differ from direct-page availability.
