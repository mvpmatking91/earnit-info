The Jozlo guide

An alarm with a next step.

Build your morning around a task you choose.

Jozlo combines an app blocker and exercise rewards with optional task-based alarms. An alarm can cue your morning routine and lead into a selected task. It is not an alarm that can remove Apple's system stop controls or prevent you from force-quitting an app.

Set up your first alarm.

  1. Open Jozlo and choose the Alarms tab.
  2. Allow alarm permission when requested. Check your iPhone's sound and relevant device settings.
  3. Create an alarm and choose the time, available sound, and task for your routine.
  4. Save a test alarm a few minutes ahead. Check the full flow on your actual phone before using it for an important morning.

Choose a useful morning task.

Start with a manageable task rather than a punishing exercise goal. Follow any setup instructions, use a stable phone position for camera verification, and leave room to move safely. Exercise counting is an estimate, not a medical assessment or exercise-form check.

If you use an exercise task, the on-device counter can show progress and speak the count. Custom cloud checks require separate consent, internet access, and a metered allowance.

Understand interruptions.

Calls, Siri, app switching, and system interruptions can pause camera verification. If Jozlo shows a paused set, return to the app and use the option to resume while keeping completed reps. Do not assume the camera stayed active during an interruption.

Know what iOS still controls.

Standard iOS alarm stop controls remain available. Force-quitting can stop app-owned background playback, and follow-up alarm coverage is finite. Permissions and device settings affect delivery. Jozlo should not be described as impossible to silence or impossible to bypass.

For an important appointment or work shift, keep a separate backup alarm. Test your saved Jozlo alarm and its task-completion behavior after changing sounds, permissions, or device settings.

Connect your morning to earned access.

Alarms provide a cue; app plans define the restriction and reward rules. Set up an earned screen time plan if you want completed tasks to unlock distracting apps. An alarm by itself should not be assumed to configure those plans automatically.

Need help with a missed alarm, sound, or a paused task? Visit support and include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps you took.
