Jozlo support

Keep your day moving.

Help with your first plan, an interrupted set, or a morning alarm.

Contact support

Email michaelpersichini@yahoo.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Jozlo version, and the steps that caused the problem. Do not send passwords, access tokens, private camera footage, or other people's information. This site does not accept uploads.

I cannot find Jozlo in App Store search.

Open the direct download link below. The current public release is available in the United States and requires iOS 26 or later. Check your Apple Account's storefront and any Screen Time content restrictions. Search results and direct-page availability can differ. If the direct link also fails, tell support your storefront, iOS version, and the exact error.

The camera paused when I used Siri.

Return to Jozlo and tap Resume to continue with your completed reps. Siri and other system interruptions can pause verification. To capture an active counter, use the side and volume-up buttons together, or start a screen recording before opening the camera.

The exercise counter is not counting.

Grant camera permission, place your phone on a stable surface, and follow the selected exercise's positioning instructions. Keep the required joints in view and wait for the countdown. On-device exercise counting is the default and does not need cloud credits. Counts can be inaccurate; cancel or reposition if needed.

A cloud or custom task will not verify.

Check the in-app AI connection, internet access, consent, and available metered allowance. Frame only the intended activity and keep private documents and other people out of view. A connection may need renewal after expiry. An uncertain result does not earn access. Cloud checks are separate from Pro.

A selected app is still locked.

Open Jozlo directly, confirm the relevant plan, complete the chosen task, and start the earned break. The iOS restriction shield does not necessarily open Jozlo. You can adjust or remove your voluntary rule in Jozlo or revoke Screen Time authorization in iPhone Settings. Keep essential apps out of blocking plans.

How do earned-time controls work?

Continuous countdowns include time spent elsewhere. App-use allowances and saved earned minutes follow the plan and available Pro controls shown in the app. Changing a plan can relock an active break. Review the on-screen explanations before saving changes.

How do I test an alarm?

Grant alarm permission and save a real alarm a few minutes ahead. Test sound, opening the routine, background behavior, and task completion on your phone. Standard iOS stop controls remain available, and force-quitting affects app-owned playback. Keep a separate backup for important mornings. Read the alarm guide.

Subscriptions and restoring purchases

In Jozlo, open Settings and Explore Jozlo Pro to find Restore Purchases and subscription management. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles billing and cancellation. Current prices and billing periods appear in the app and Apple purchase sheet.

TestFlight testers

If you are using a TestFlight build, open TestFlight, select Jozlo, and use Send Beta Feedback. Public App Store users can use the support email above.
