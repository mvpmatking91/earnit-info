EarnIt beta support

Contact the developer

When you receive the TestFlight beta, open TestFlight, select EarnIt, and choose Send Beta Feedback. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Do not include passwords, access tokens, private footage or other people’s information. This page does not accept uploads.

AI will not connect or verify

Check internet access and camera permission, then use Settings → Connect AI. A fresh connection may be needed after expiry. Frame the activity clearly and keep the camera view continuous. An uncertain result does not unlock your target. Limits and service outages can interrupt verification; cancel the check and adjust your voluntary rule if necessary.

A selected app is still locked

Open EarnIt directly to complete its tasks; the iOS shield does not automatically open it. You can remove a rule in EarnIt or revoke its Screen Time authorization in system settings. Essential phone functions should never be selected for blocking.

Timed access and website coverage

Rewards count down continuously after verification, including time spent elsewhere. iOS rewards currently range from 15 to 120 minutes. Reblocking depends on system callbacks and is under device testing. Android website filtering supports specific browser address bars and does not cover every browser or in-app web view.

Alarm help

Grant the separate alarm permission, set a nearby test alarm, and check sound and task behavior on your phone. Use emergency stop when needed. Until device testing is complete, keep a reliable backup alarm.

Beta status

No public App Store release is available yet. The optional alarm feature currently requires iOS 26 or newer. A public support channel will be added before general release.
