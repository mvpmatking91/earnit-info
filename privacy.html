EarnIt beta privacy notice · Updated October 7, 2026

On-device exercise checks

Exercise checks default to on-device pose detection. Camera frames are processed in memory to estimate body joints and count complete movement cycles. In this mode, no camera frames, joint arrays or task checks are sent to our service or OpenAI, and no photo or video file is written. A local completion record stores the task, goal, count, duration and random attempt identifier. Camera access and explicit consent are required. The counter can make mistakes; it does not assess health, safe exercise form or identity.

Cloud live checks

Other visible tasks and optional cloud exercise checks use sampled camera frames, with separate explicit consent. Frames, task identifiers, rep goals, timing and an optional custom completion criterion are sent over HTTPS to our verification service. For cloud exercise checks, body-joint positions are also sent to recompute cycles. OpenAI receives sampled images and task instructions, not joint arrays. No audio is collected. EarnIt does not save recordings on your phone, retain frames on its backend or log request bodies. This is sampled-frame assessment, not continuous video streaming. Keep other people, private documents and sensitive surroundings out of view.

AI processing

OpenAI processes frames and task instructions to assess visible completion. We request no stored Responses API result using store:false. This does not eliminate OpenAI abuse-monitoring retention, which can ordinarily include content for up to 30 days, with policy exceptions. Provider retention is outside the app’s local deletion controls. Read OpenAI’s data controls.

What stays on your phone

Selected app tokens, target domains, alarm settings and progress history stay on your device and are not sent to the AI service. Task identifiers and selected rep goals are sent only for requested AI verification. On iOS, Screen Time uses opaque app selections. On Android, supported browser address bars are checked locally to match only the domains you chose. We do not collect page content, messages or browsing history. Local exports contain progress and settings; they exclude AI tokens and camera footage.

AI connection and service records

No email is required for beta AI connections. The service stores a random install identifier, a hashed access token and daily counters. Connections expire after 30 days. Expired records are removed on subsequent enrollment; daily counters older than seven days are cleaned during requests. For up to one hour, the service caches verification results and a hash of the submitted evidence to recover lost responses. These records contain verdicts, task identifiers, bounded observations, completion receipts and accounting metadata, but no camera images or joint arrays. A custom criterion can be reflected in the model’s response. Do not include private information in it. Short-lived challenge identifiers prevent reuse of completed checks; expired challenge records are cleaned on new task checks. These records do not contain camera frames. Render hosts published pages and any deployed service and may process connection information such as IP addresses for delivery and security.

Measured usage and rewards

On iOS, usage summaries are displayed inside Apple’s DeviceActivity report extension and are not exported to the app’s JavaScript or the backend. On Android, optional Usage Access provides local totals for selected apps. App usage allowances are consumed using local foreground-app events, excluding the lock screen and EarnIt’s blocking overlay. Reward balances, daily allowances, schedules and technical issue categories stay on your phone. We do not claim these totals measure time saved.

Your controls

Use Settings to delete your AI connection while online, then delete local app data. Server connection deletion revokes that token and removes its per-install counters. Uninstalling removes local app storage but does not itself send a server deletion request. You can revoke camera, Screen Time or Accessibility permissions through phone settings. Removing a selected target removes its restriction. Camera checks can be cancelled without earning access.

Purpose and limits

Beta participation is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The app is a voluntary habit tool, not medical care or an unbypassable lock. No analytics or advertising SDK has been added to this project.

Questions

Beta testers can contact the developer using TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback feature. See our support page. A public support contact must be established before an App Store release.
