Your progress. Your privacy.

Jozlo beta privacy notice · Updated October 27, 2026

On-device exercise checks

Exercise checks default to on-device pose detection. Camera frames are processed in memory to estimate body joints and count complete movement cycles. In this mode, no camera frames, joint arrays or task checks are sent to our service or OpenAI, and no photo or video file is written unless you separately enable optional recording. A local completion record stores the task, goal, count, duration and random attempt identifier. Camera access and explicit consent are required. The counter can make mistakes; it does not assess health, safe exercise form or identity.

On-device detection SDK

Jozlo uses Google ML Kit for on-device pose detection. Google does not receive camera images or pose results from this SDK. ML Kit sends performance and usage diagnostics, including device and app information, a per-installation identifier, latency, API configuration, event types and error codes, to Google. Google uses these metrics to operate, debug and improve its APIs and detect misuse. This technical SDK data is separate from the local task history and is not removed by Jozlo’s local deletion controls. See Google ML Kit terms and privacy and Google’s SDK data disclosure.

Cloud live checks

Other visible tasks and optional cloud exercise checks use sampled camera frames, with separate explicit consent. Frames, task identifiers, rep goals, timing and an optional custom completion criterion are sent over HTTPS to our verification service. For cloud exercise checks, body-joint positions are also sent to recompute cycles. OpenAI receives sampled images and task instructions, not joint arrays. No audio is collected. Cloud checks do not save a video recording, retain frames on Jozlo’s backend or log request bodies. This is sampled-frame assessment, not continuous video streaming. Keep other people, private documents and sensitive surroundings out of view.

Custom task setup

When you choose Find a way to verify, your task description is sent over HTTPS to our service and OpenAI to propose a camera-verifiable task or explain why it is unsupported. Do not include personal details or sensitive documents. Setup uses the same metered cloud allowance as other AI requests. The backend does not save task descriptions or log request bodies for this planner. The accepted description, completion criterion, camera guidance and signed proposal are saved on your phone with the app plan and may appear in your local export. The criterion and camera guidance are sent again when you start a live check. The signed proposal is readable data, not encrypted storage, and expires after one year; analyze the task again if it expires. Provider retention rules still apply. Proposed verification is an estimate of feasibility and can fail; it does not guarantee that completion can be proved.

Optional videos and social sharing

Recording is off by default. For on-device rep tasks, you may choose to record a temporary, silent video of your setup and completed set, up to five minutes. It stays in the app’s local cache and is not sent to Jozlo or OpenAI for verification. You can review the clip before choosing to share it. Jozlo deletes its temporary copy when you finish the review or next reopen the app. Cancelled or interrupted recordings are discarded; a recording left by an unexpected termination is cleared on the next launch. The phone’s share menu sends only the content you select to your chosen app. Jozlo does not publish automatically. Copies you post or save in another app follow that app’s privacy, audience and retention settings and are not removed by Jozlo’s deletion controls.

Progress posts and challenges

Sharing completion cards, captions and challenge invitations is optional. Shared content can include the exercise, completed count, invitation date and random challenge identifier. It does not include your restricted-app selections or AI connection token. Anyone who receives an invitation code may join while it is valid, including when you post it publicly. Rep results are counted and saved on your phone; invitation codes do not provide independently authenticated cross-user rankings.

AI processing

OpenAI processes frames and task instructions to assess visible completion. We request no stored Responses API result using store:false. This does not eliminate OpenAI abuse-monitoring retention, which can ordinarily include content for up to 30 days, with policy exceptions. Provider retention is outside the app’s local deletion controls. Read OpenAI’s data controls.

What stays on your phone

Selected app tokens, target domains, alarm settings and progress history stay on your device and are not sent to the AI service. Task identifiers and selected rep goals are sent only for requested AI verification. On iOS, Screen Time uses opaque app selections. On Android, supported browser address bars are checked locally to match only the domains you chose. We do not collect page content, messages or browsing history. Local exports contain progress and settings; they exclude AI tokens and camera footage.

AI connection and service records

No email is required for beta AI connections. The service stores a random install identifier, a hashed access token and daily counters. Connections expire after 30 days. Expired records are removed on subsequent enrollment; daily counters older than seven days are cleaned during requests. For up to one hour, the service caches verification results and a hash of the submitted evidence to recover lost responses. These records contain verdicts, task identifiers, bounded observations, completion receipts and accounting metadata, but no camera images or joint arrays. A custom criterion can be reflected in the model’s response. Do not include private information in it. Short-lived challenge identifiers prevent reuse of completed checks; expired challenge records are cleaned on new task checks. These records do not contain camera frames. Render hosts published pages and any deployed service and may process connection information such as IP addresses for delivery and security.

Measured usage and rewards

On iOS, usage summaries are displayed inside Apple’s DeviceActivity report extension and are not exported to the app’s JavaScript or the backend. On Android, optional Usage Access provides local totals for selected apps. App usage allowances are consumed using local foreground-app events, excluding the lock screen and Jozlo’s blocking overlay. Reward balances, daily allowances, schedules and technical issue categories stay on your phone. We do not claim these totals measure time saved.

Your controls

Use Settings to delete your AI connection while online, then delete local app data. Server connection deletion revokes that token and removes its per-install counters. Uninstalling removes local app storage but does not itself send a server deletion request. You can revoke camera, Screen Time or Accessibility permissions through phone settings. Removing a selected target removes its restriction. Camera checks can be cancelled without earning access.

Purpose and limits

Beta participation is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The app is a voluntary habit tool, not medical care or an unbypassable lock. No advertising SDK has been added. Google ML Kit’s technical diagnostics are described above.

Questions

Beta testers can contact the developer using TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback feature. See our support page. A public support contact must be established before an App Store release.
